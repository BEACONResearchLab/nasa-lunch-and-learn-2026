# NASA Lunch and Learn 2026: Ocean Satellite Data for Coral Reefs

A hands-on introduction to NASA satellite data for undergraduate STEM students, hosted by BEACON Research Lab. No coding experience needed.

In about 45 minutes you will make two maps of Florida's coastal ocean from real NASA data:

- **Sea surface temperature** from GHRSST MUR (1 km)
- **Chlorophyll-a** from the PACE satellite (4 km)

## Why this matters for coral reefs

Corals bleach when the water stays too warm for too long, and they do best in clear, low-nutrient water. Satellites measure both temperature and water color every day, across every U.S. reef region. Our project uses these data to study reefs in Florida, Hawaiʻi, and Puerto Rico.
